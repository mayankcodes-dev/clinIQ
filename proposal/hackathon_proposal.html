1. Problem Statement

Indian government hospitals serve 4,000–10,000 OPD patients daily with 2–5 minute consultations. Doctors spend 60–70% of that time asking history instead of examining patients. India has 1 doctor per 1,511 people — every second of doctor time is critical.

The problem is worse for non-English speaking, low-literacy, elderly, and rural patients who cannot fill digital forms. AYUSH practitioners face additional complexity — Dashavidha Pariksha requires 10+ constitutional parameters that cannot be captured manually under time pressure.

ABDM has built the digital health infrastructure, but there is no patient-facing DPI application that captures structured clinical history in 22 Indian languages and feeds it into the ABDM ecosystem.

2. Proposed Solution

ClinIQ is an AI-powered kiosk application that conducts the entire clinical history interview autonomously, in the patient's own language, before they enter the consultation room. It produces a doctor-ready clinical summary in standard medical format.

How it works:

  1. Patient selects language (22 Indian languages) → authenticates via ABHA ID / Aadhaar OTP / Mobile OTP
  2. Gives ABDM-compliant consent (with Bhashini TTS audio explanation in their language)
  3. Chooses OPD type — General Medicine or AYUSH
  4. AI conducts adaptive clinical interview via voice (Bhashini ASR) + guided touch chips
  5. Scans existing prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries (Gemini Vision OCR)
  6. AI generates structured summary — Chief Complaint, HPI, PMHx, Drug/Allergy, Family, Personal, ROS
  7. Doctor sees pre-filled report on dashboard → spends time on examination, not history-taking
  8. Session ends → all PII cleared from device (DPDP Act 2023 compliant)

Key features:

  • Adaptive SOCRATES-framework questioning with real-time red-flag emergency detection
  • AYUSH Dashavidha Pariksha — all 10 constitutional parameters (Prakriti through Vaya)
  • Medical document OCR with dosage normalization and abnormal-value highlighting
  • Triple AI fallback (Groq → Gemini → Offline) — interview never breaks, even without internet
  • Professional A4 print-ready clinical report with ICD-10 suggestions

5. Innovation & Scalability

What makes it unique:

  • First DPI-native clinical history application — integrates ABDM + Bhashini + ABHA + FHIR R4 simultaneously. The missing patient-facing layer in India's digital health stack.
  • Voice-first, not form-first — patients talk naturally; AI listens, probes deeper using SOCRATES framework, and structures the response into standard clinical format.
  • AYUSH as a first-class mode — Dashavidha Pariksha with all 10 parameters has dedicated interview stages. No existing system supports this.
  • Triple-fallback AI — Groq → Gemini → Offline clinically vetted questions in 11 languages. Works even with zero internet.
  • Red-flag emergency detection — real-time multilingual pattern matching for cardiac, stroke, bleeding, and psychiatric emergencies.

Scalability: ClinIQ is a PWA — runs on any device with a browser. A hospital deploys by opening a URL on an existing tablet. Bhashini and ABDM APIs are free for public sector use, so incremental cost per deployment is near zero. Can scale from a single hospital pilot to 10,000+ PHCs/CHCs across India.
